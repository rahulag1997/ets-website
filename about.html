About

Software that gets out of your way

Easy Tech Solutions builds a small line of business software with one aim: make it genuinely easy.

Why we exist

A lot of business software has drifted away from the people who actually use it — more dashboards, more upsells, more of your data living on someone else's servers. Small businesses end up paying monthly for tools they half-understand and can't easily leave.

We think it can be simpler. Our products are built to be easy in three specific ways.

What "easy" means to us

  • Easy to learn. Sensible defaults, plain language, and screens that do what they look like they do.
  • Easy to trust. Offline-first. Your data lives on your device, not in our account system. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to hand over.
  • Easy to own. No mandatory subscription to get started, and your data is yours to export whenever you want.

How we work

Small team, small product line, long-term support. We'd rather do a few things well than many things badly — so we ship a product, then keep improving it based on how people really use it.
