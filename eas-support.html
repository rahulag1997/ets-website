EAS Support

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Help for Easy Accounting Solutions on Android, iOS, and desktop (macOS, Windows, Linux).

Contact us

Email support@etsproducts.in. Please include:

  • your platform — Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, or Linux;
  • the app version, from the About screen;
  • what you were doing when the problem happened, and what you expected instead.

We aim to reply within a few business days.

Common questions

I forgot my company password

It cannot be recovered. Your local data and your backups are encrypted with a key derived from that password, and we keep no copy of it. If you have a recent backup whose password you know, restore from it; otherwise you will need to set up the company again.

How do I back up my data?

Open Backup & Restore in the app and export a backup. The file is encrypted with your company password. Copy it somewhere safe — another drive, or your own cloud storage — and do this regularly.

How do I move to a new device?

Export a backup on the old device, transfer the file to the new one, install EAS there, and use Restore. Restore always creates a fresh company, so nothing you already have is overwritten.

How do I import data from other software?

Use the CSV import step when creating a new company. Templates for accounts, items, bills, and purchases are available on that screen.

Does EAS work offline?

Yes, entirely. It makes no network connection for your data.

What data does EAS collect about me?

None. See the EAS Privacy Policy.
