Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Short version: this website does not use cookies, analytics, or trackers, and our apps keep your data on your own device. We don't run an account system and we don't sell data.

Scope

This policy covers the Easy Tech Solutions website and the applications we publish (currently Easy Accounting Solutions; Easy Camera Solutions is in development and this policy will be updated before it ships).

The website

  • The site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or tracking pixels.
  • We do not run analytics and do not build profiles of visitors.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers process the technical information needed to deliver a page (such as your IP address and browser user-agent) and may retain it briefly in security logs. This is handled under GitHub's own privacy practices and is not shared with us in an identifiable form.
  • If you email us using an address on this site, we receive whatever you put in that email and use it only to reply.

The apps

  • Easy Accounting Solutions stores all of your business data in a local database on your own device. It has no account system and does not transmit your accounting data to us or to any third party.
  • Backups you create stay where you save them. A backup file is encrypted with a key derived from your company password.
  • The apps do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • If a future version adds an optional online feature (for example cloud sync), it will be opt-in and this policy will be updated to describe exactly what it does.

Data we hold about you

Essentially none. We have no user database. The only personal data we might hold is the content of an email you chose to send us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We only share information where required by law.

Children

Our products are business tools and are not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
