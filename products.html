EAS

Easy Accounting Solutions

A complete accounting workspace for small businesses that runs entirely on your own device.

No account to create, no data to upload, no subscription to start. Everything is stored in a local database on your computer or phone, and the app works fully offline.

  • Invoicing and billing
  • Expense and cashflow tracking (receipts & payments)
  • Bills, purchases and inventory
  • GST reporting with CGST / SGST / IGST breakdown
  • Manual journal vouchers for adjustments and transfers
  • Encrypted local backup and restore
  • CSV import to bring data over from your previous software

Platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS

Status: in active development. Availability details coming soon.

App legal: Privacy · Terms of Use · Support
