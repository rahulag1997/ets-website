EAS

Easy Accounting Solutions

Offline-first bookkeeping and GST invoicing for small businesses — your data lives on your device, and stays there, unless you choose to back it up or sync it.

No account needed to use the core app. Every calculation happens on your device; an optional Cloud sync and ETS Account add-on is there only if you want a phone and a desktop kept in sync.

  • Bills and purchases with itemised lines, HSN codes and GST rates, plus automatic CGST/SGST vs IGST split
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B export in the government offline-tool format
  • Quotations that convert straight into a bill, and recurring bill templates
  • Expenses, receipts & payments, and post-dated cheque tracking (pending → cleared/bounced)
  • Inventory with weighted-average cost and landed-cost profit per item
  • Six live financial reports: P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Day Book, Aged Receivables/Payables, Cash Flow
  • Manual journal vouchers, multi-company support, period lock and a full audit log
  • CSV import to bring data over from your previous software
  • Encrypted local backup and restore, free and account-free; optional hosted Cloud backup & sync as a paid add-on

Platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS

Status: in active development — currently in Apple App Store review. Availability details coming soon.

EIS

Easy Image Solutions

EIS bundles the image jobs you do all the time into one small, ad-free app that works completely offline.

No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no account, and no network access — your images never leave your device, and the file you start from is never overwritten; every tool writes a new file.

  • Crop — free or fixed aspect ratios
  • Resize — exact pixels, a percentage, or fit the longest edge
  • Compress — hit a target file size with a live quality preview
  • Convert between JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and TIFF
  • Merge images into a strip, a grid, or a PDF
  • Generate QR codes — links, UPI, Wi-Fi, text, email, phone, SMS
  • Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and hue
  • Export a logo as a favicon and full app-icon set
  • View and strip EXIF metadata
  • Watermark with text or a logo, anchored, with opacity

Platforms: Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux

Status: available now on iOS. Android and desktop builds coming soon.

ECS

Easy Camera Solutions

See every camera on your network in one place — and, if you want it, know who showed up and when.

No cloud subscription, no footage leaving your network. ECS connects directly to your IP cameras over RTSP and runs entirely on your own computer.

  • Live multi-camera grid, click to view one full-screen
  • Automatic camera discovery on your local network
  • Auto-reconnect if a camera drops
  • Manual snapshots
  • Pro: person detection with desktop notifications
  • Pro: face identification — label a known person once, get notified by name after

Platforms: macOS. Windows build exists but is not yet verified on real hardware.

Status: available now for macOS — Lite is free, Pro adds detection.
